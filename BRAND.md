# The Charm City Coin

**A media brand about business in Baltimore.** It started as Charm City B2B and was renamed on Oct 4, 2026. Same mission, same team, same voice. It can grow into whatever comes next.

## What we are
**TBPN for Baltimore business.** TBPN covers the tech industry every day with obsession and commitment. We cover Baltimore business the same way: every deal, opening, contract, filing, event, and founder story, from the corner store to the $40M contractor. Nothing is too small to cover, and nothing gets covered without receipts.

## Where we come from
Baltimore has been publishing business news for over 200 years. We're the newest link in that chain.

| Inspiration | What we take from it |
|---|---|
| **Niles' Weekly Register** (Hezekiah Niles, Baltimore, from 1811) | One of the country's first publications about commerce and the economy. Business news as a public record people come back to every week. |
| **The Afro-American (The AFRO)** | Coverage from inside the community, for the community. Black business and Black Baltimore treated as the main story, not a sidebar. |
| **The Baltimore Sun** | The paper of record for the city. Depth, consistency, showing up every day. |
| **The Daily Record** | Business, legal, and real estate as their own beat. The details owners actually use. |
| **City Paper** | Voice, attitude, and an independent eye on the city. |
| **TBPN** | The modern format: daily, personality-led, fast, and obsessed with its industry. |

## How that shows up in the work
- **Veracity.** Every claim has a source a reader can open. If it isn't confirmed, we say so or we don't run it.
- **Obsession.** We cover all of Baltimore business, every size and every industry, every week.
- **Commitment.** Daily posts, a weekly short, a weekly show, and two newsletters a week. We keep showing up.
- **Personality.** Q's take is the product. News without a point of view is what aggregators do.

## What stayed the same
The writers, the agents, the loop, the rules, and Q's brand voice (`voice/`) are all unchanged. Only the name changed.
