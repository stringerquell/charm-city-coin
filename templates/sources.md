# Baltimore source list

Scout and Radar read this. Built from the Strategy Doc (Radar sources, Sept 27) and the Media Block research rules.

**First Scout run:** check each link still works and note how to pull it (RSS, search page, data API, PDF agenda). Mark the Status column. Add sources here as the Listener finds them.

## Tier 1: primary public records (Radar)

| Source | What it gives us | Where | Cadence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maryland SDAT / Business Express | New business filings (Baltimore City + County) | egov.maryland.gov/BusinessExpress | Weekly | verify |
| Open Baltimore | Building permits, other city datasets | data.baltimorecity.gov | Weekly | verify |
| Baltimore City Liquor Board (BLLC) | New liquor license applications = openings | llc.baltimorecity.gov | Weekly | verify |
| Board of Estimates | Contract awards, agendas | comptroller.baltimorecity.gov (Board of Estimates) | Weekly (Wednesday meetings) | verify |
| CitiBuy | City bids and solicitations | citibuy.baltimorecity.gov | Daily scan | verify |
| eMMA (Maryland Marketplace) | State bids and awards | emma.maryland.gov | Daily scan | verify |
| Businesses for sale | Listings in Baltimore metro | BizBuySell and similar (public listing pages only) | Weekly | verify |

## Rules
- Paywalled: headline and public facts only, never copy text, credit "via [outlet]."
- Every item needs a link a reader can open.
- Public data only. Never add a person to any list from these sources.
